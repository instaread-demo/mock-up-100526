Midwich and Resi Media Announce U.S. Distribution Partnership

Justin DeMois
August 3, 2026
5 min read

Partnership expands access to Resi’s streaming solutions and creates new growth opportunities across house of worship, education, corporate, government, live entertainment, and pro AV sectors

Chesterton, IN and Allen, TX – August 3, 2026 – Midwich, a leading distributor of unified communications, broadcast, and professional AV solutions, and Resi Media, a pioneer in resilient video delivery technology, today announced a strategic distribution partnership that will expand access to Resi’s solutions through Midwich’s extensive network of systems integrators and value-added resellers across the United States. 

Resi has appointed Midwich as its U.S. distribution partner, reinforcing both companies’ commitment to expanding commercial reach and delivering greater value to systems integrators and resellers nationwide. The partnership provides Resi partners with enhanced access to complementary technologies, financing options, and an expanded portfolio of solutions to better serve their customers. Additionally, Resi’s industry-leading video delivery technology is now available to Midwich and its network.

Resi has established itself as a trusted provider of streaming and video delivery solutions, where its patented Resilient Streaming Protocol (RSP) and cloud-based platform help organizations deliver reliable, high-quality live and on-demand video experiences. Through Midwich’s value-added distribution model, Resi partners will now have access to a broader ecosystem of technologies, including cameras, networking solutions, storage, audio systems, displays, and production equipment.

“This partnership represents a natural alignment between two organizations focused on helping channel partners deliver exceptional experiences to their customers,” said Rich D’Angelo, Executive Director of Business Management – Broadcast for Midwich. “Resi has built an outstanding reputation within the Live Production community and has developed a highly engaged reseller network. We are excited to welcome these partners to Midwich and provide them with access to the technologies, resources, and solutions needed to grow their businesses. We also see tremendous opportunity to help Resi expand its reach into additional adjacencies that value high-quality, reliable streaming such as education, corporate, government, live entertainment, and other pro AV verticals where Midwich and our partners have established relationships and expertise.”

In addition to supporting Resi’s established leadership position within the house of worship space, the companies will collaborate on strategic go-to-market initiatives designed to accelerate adoption in new industries and create additional growth opportunities for channel partners. Together, Midwich and Resi will leverage their complementary strengths to help partners deliver complete solutions and capitalize on new business opportunities.

“This partnership reflects a genuine exchange of value,” said Matthew Smith, General Manager, Resi Media. “Midwich provides our partners with access to an established distribution network and the market credibility needed to accelerate our expansion into education, corporate, government, and live entertainment. In turn, Midwich’s network gains access to a patented, resilient streaming technology that its integrators can offer their customers with confidence.”

“We are thrilled to partner with Midwich as we enter the next phase of our channel strategy,” said Matt Groves, Director of Business Development, Resi Media. “Midwich brings an exceptional combination of industry expertise, channel relationships, and complementary technologies that will help our partners grow and deliver even greater value to their customers. This partnership allows us to strengthen our commitment to the house of worship sector while accelerating our expansion into new customer segments. We are confident that Midwich will be an exceptional extension of the Resi team.”

By combining Midwich’s extensive portfolio of professional AV, broadcast, and unified communications technologies with Resi’s video delivery platform, both organizations can now offer complete, end-to-end customer solutions. Working together, the two companies will align their go-to-market teams, streamline distribution workflows, and deliver specialized support to empower integrators across key commercial sectors.

For more information about Resi Media, visit www.resi.io. For more information about Midwich US, visit www.midwich.us.

About Resi Media

Resi Media is a leading provider of resilient streaming and video delivery solutions designed to help organizations reliably broadcast live events to audiences around the world. A Pushpay company, Resi is powered by its patented Resilient Streaming Protocol (RSP), Resi’s platform enables churches, educational institutions, government organizations, corporations, and live entertainment venues to deliver high-quality live and on-demand video experiences with unmatched reliability. For more information, visit www.resi.io.

About Midwich

We are Midwich, your AV solutions experts in the U.S. and part of the global Midwich Group. Formerly known as Starin, The Farm AV, and Toolfarm, we’ve unified under one name to reflect a stronger, more connected presence with expanded capabilities across unified communications, broadcast, and professional AV. This evolution marks a new chapter built on decades of expertise and a shared commitment to helping our customers succeed. 

As a trusted partner, Midwich is known for reliability, responsiveness, and a solutions-first mindset. Our team brings deep technical knowledge and hands-on experience, supporting everything from solution development to complex system design. We offer a robust portfolio of best-in-class technology partners and deliver scalable services through Midwich Plus. With enhanced broadcast capabilities from top-tier brands and a full-service UC offering, we’re equipped to support the full spectrum of your AV needs. 

As a team laser-focused on collaborating closely with our customers to tailor solutions, provide training, and offer end-to-end support, our goal isn’t to be the biggest – it’s to be the most trusted. Backed by the strength of the global Midwich Group, we bring international expertise with a personal, local touch to help our customers create impactful communications and unforgettable experiences. 

At Midwich, we’re passionate about technology, collaboration, and pushing boundaries to help you deliver positive business outcomes and innovate for tomorrow. 

Learn more at www.midwich.us.
