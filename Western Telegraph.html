Roads were closed in Pembrokeshire over the weekend following incidents that required police response.

Dyfed-Powys Police shut the B4318 between Sageston and Heatherton on Saturday night after a collision.

An alert was issued at 9.15pm on October 3, advising motorists to avoid the area and use alternative routes.

No details have been released about the number of vehicles involved or whether anyone was injured.

The road remained closed for several hours before reopening shortly after midnight.

In an update issued at 12.16am on Sunday, October 4, Dyfed-Powys Police thanked motorists for their patience and confirmed the route was once again open.

Elsewhere, the A40 between Haverfordwest and Wolfscastle was also closed but later reopened.

Police confirmed on Sunday morning that traffic was moving normally on the route.

The Western Telegraph has contacted Dyfed-Powys Police for more information about both incidents, including whether there were any injuries or arrests, but had not received a response at the time of publication.
