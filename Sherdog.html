Glover Teixeira: Raoni Barcelos had brain swelling after UFC Vegas 121

Sayan Nag Oct 5, 2026
Glover Teixeira has shared more details about Raoni Barcelos’ concerning defeat to Raul Rosas Jr.

Barcelos took on Rosas Jr. in a five-round Ultimate Fighting Championship bantamweight bout headlining UFC Vegas 121 at Meta Apex in Las Vegas on Sept. 26. Barcelos, who was ahead on the judges’ scorecards going into the final round, suddenly went limp during a wrestling exchange and lost consciousness, prompting the referee to stop the fight.

Advertisement

Teixeira says Barcelos underwent neurological testing after losing consciousness against Raul Rosas Jr.


Rosas Jr. was declared the winner by technical knockout, while Barcelos was rushed to a Level 1 trauma center. Former UFC champ Teixeira was in Barcelos’ corner for the fight against Rosas Jr., and he recently shared some concerning details about Barcelos’ medical reports. Teixeira said Barcelos was tested for neurological issues because it wasn’t a conventional knockout.

“It was different because it wasn’t a knockout,” Teixeira said on the Overdogs Brasil podcast. “If it had been a knockout, we’re used to that. A fighter takes a shot, gets knocked out, OK, let’s take him to the hospital, all right. But this was something that happened out of nowhere, and we started thinking about all kinds of things. Could it have been his heart? Could it have been something neurological? They went in and ran the tests.”

According to Teixeira, some swelling was found in Barcelos’ brain, which might have caused him to lose consciousness. Teixeira speculated that it might have been the body’s defense mechanism to make an exhausted Barcelos stop.

“There was some swelling in his brain, so it was a traumatic brain injury and maybe that swelling caused him to black out,” Teixeira added. “It was the body’s defense mechanism. ‘Let’s shut this down because this guy isn’t going to stop.’ His mind was stronger than his body, right? He didn’t want to give up.”
