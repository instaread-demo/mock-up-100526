Highway to Bell Stack & Collect™ is now available for our B2B partners!

We’re turning up the volume through the halls of the Home of Games as we welcome the arrival of the online casino game, Highway to Bell Stack & Collect™! This hard-rocking Hell-Rock slot is now available to our B2B partners.

 

Blazing Fireball symbols light up the 5 reels and 20 win lines, and whenever they land alongside the Bell Collector, the Bell Collect activates. Miss the Bell? The Mystery Bell Collect can randomly step in at any time, sending those Fireball values flying toward the giant Bell outside the reels for a surprise payout.

 

If that wasn’t enough to get players into a hellishly good winning sweat, then unleash the Stack & Collect™ Bonus, where Fireballs keep stacking and Incrementers boost their values for big win potential as players progress towards the grand prize!

 

This game also includes a Buy Bonus option in certain licences, allowing players to jump straight into the Stack & Collect™ Bonus.
