Resolve invites people to join three walks from Letchworth

Resolve is inviting people to participate in its 'Ramble for Resolve' event in Letchworth.

The event, which will take place on October 17, offers three different walks: a 10km Letchworth circular walk, a 25km walk from Letchworth to Meldreth, and a 45km ultra-walk from Letchworth to Cambridge.

The walks are being organised in partnership with Letchworth company Challenge Central, who will provide van support, designated stops, guides, and baggage transfers.

The event is aimed at raising awareness about the work done by Resolve, a charity that provides drug and alcohol recovery services and homeless and rough sleeping support in Letchworth and Welwyn Hatfield.

Gary Sutcliffe, fundraising manager at Resolve, said: "I’m really hoping we can encourage lots of people to come and join us and choose and sign up for one of the three amazing walks Challenge Central are putting on for us.

"We can talk a little about fundraising when you sign up but we mainly just want people to look at one of the lovely walks as they will be across the countryside and have a think about an autumn challenge they might want to give a go."

An events village is also planned for Howard Park and Gardens, promising an exciting day for all participants.

For more details, visit the Ramble for Resolve 2026 page on the Challenge Central website.
