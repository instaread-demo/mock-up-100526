Child Growth Chart: Average Weight and Height by Age

Reviewed by Rachel Gregory, Writer, Editor and Project Manager

We all know that kids come in all shapes and sizes, but the way your little one is growing can sometimes offer some insight into their health. Your baby’s height and weight will go through rapid change over their first two years, and you may wonder if they’re growing as they should be, and how they compare to other babies.

To give you a benchmark, more than half of kids fall within the ranges we’re giving. 15 per cent of kids fall above these numbers and 15 per cent of kids below them.

Keep in mind that while our growth chart offers guidelines that apply to more than half of all Canadian children, what matters most is that your child is following a pattern that's right for them. If your child's growth is too fast or too slow, it may point to a problem with health or nutrition (Dietitians of Canada 2023).

At each doctor appointment, they'll weigh and measure your child and tell you their height and weight percentiles. Make sure you talk to doctor about any concerns you may have about your child's growth.
