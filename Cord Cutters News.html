Plex’s New Social Features Are Now Live on Android TV

Plex is expanding its social features again, this time to Android TV. The update brings Discussions, Lists, Reviews, Reactions and Follows to another major big-screen platform.

The launch on Android TV follows a rollout on Apple TV in September after initially launching on Roku and Fire TV. We first covered Plex’s new social features in June when the company announced plans to make streaming discovery more of a community experience.

Plex Users Are Already Socializing on the Big Screen

Plex’s social tools let users discover movies and shows through other people’s activity instead of relying solely on streaming-service algorithms. The feature set includes Discussions, Lists, Reviews, Reactions, and Follows, giving users ways to talk about what they’re watching, create and share lists, review titles, react to them, and follow people or content.

By recommending content across multiple services rather than just its own catalog, Plex stands out from most streaming apps. With the added social discovery, users can use the same app to find something to watch, see what other Plex users think about a title, follow content and people, and interact with activity from their friends and the wider Plex community.

Now with the rollout to Apple TV, Plex is meeting more users on the big screen directly from the TV app. The company says 59% of reactions and 47% of watchlist additions already happen on TV. Its discovery features have also picked up significant activity on Roku and Fire TV. On Roku alone, Plex users created more than 57,000 reactions and 24,000 lists over the past three months.

Sign Up for Plex Remote Watch Pass or Plex Pass

You can use Plex with a Remote Watch Pass, which costs $2.99 per month. This the plan for you if you don’t need the full Plex Pass feature set and want a cost-effective way to stream your media — or the libraries of friends and family — from anywhere, on any device.

A Plex Pass turns the app into a true home-entertainment hub. The company’s premium subscription is for people who want more from their personal Plex Media Server, adding features such as DVR and recording with a compatible antenna and tuner, hardware-accelerated transcoding, offline downloads, and other advanced features, including premium playback and library tools.

A Plex Pass is available with four plans:

  • Monthly: $6.99 per month
  • Annual: $69.99 per year
  • 5-Year: $249.99 for 5 years
  • Lifetime: $749.99 billed once
